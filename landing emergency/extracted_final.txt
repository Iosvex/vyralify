Q1. What exactly is Vyralify?
Vyralify is an AI-powered toolkit for Instagram creators. It helps you build Instagram pages,
discover what's trending in your niche, create content, automate your DMs and comments,
and sell products — all in one platform. Think of it as your complete Instagram business
operating system.
Q2. Do I need an existing Instagram page to use Vyralify?
No — and this is exactly why we built the Page Builder. If you're starting from zero, Vyralify
guides you through choosing a profitable niche, writing your bio, setting up your page, and
creating your first content. You can start without any Instagram page at all.
Q3. Is the automation safe? Will it get my account banned?
Vyralify's automation is built to stay within Meta's official platform guidelines. We use rate-
limiting and safe messaging patterns — the same approach used by ManyChat, which


=== PAGE 24 ===
millions of Instagram accounts use safely. We never use bots, fake engagement, or methods
that violate Instagram's terms.
Q4. What makes Vyralify AI different from just using ChatGPT?
ChatGPT gives generic responses. Vyralify AI is connected to your real Instagram data —
your actual reach, engagement, content history, and niche benchmarks. When you ask it
why a Reel underperformed, it looks at your specific numbers, not industry averages. It also
links directly to the relevant Vyralify tool to take action immediately.
Q5. Can I use Vyralify for multiple Instagram pages?
Yes. Free plan: 1 page. Pro: up to 3 pages. Elite: unlimited pages. Each page has its own
analytics, automations, and store, all switchable from one dashboard.
Q6. What happens when I hit my AI credit limit?
You'll see a notification when you reach 80% of your daily AI credits. At 100%, AI features
pause until your next daily reset. You can upgrade to Pro (300/day) or Elite (unlimited) at
any time from your account settings.
Q7. What niches does Vyralify support?
At launch, Vyralify supports 8 major niche categories: Business & Money, Self-Improvement,
Health & Fitness, Fashion & Beauty, Cars & Automotive, Travel & Lifestyle, Entertainment,
and Sports — with 40+ sub-niches total. The Discover feed, Viral Database, and AI
recommendations are all filtered by your chosen niche.
Q8. How does the Store & Commerce feature work?
You get a link-in-bio storefront at vyralify.io/your-username. Add products (digital files,
services, coaching, subscriptions), set your prices, and share the link in your Instagram bio.
When someone buys, Vyralify handles the checkout, payment processing, and automatic
product delivery — you don't need to do anything manually.
Q9. Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. You can cancel from your account settings at any time — no calls required, no hoops to
jump through. Your paid plan stays active until the end of your current billing period, then
you roll back to Free automatically.
Q10. Is my Instagram data safe with Vyralify?
Yes. We access your account only through Instagram's official OAuth system with the
minimum permissions required. We never store your Instagram password, never post
without your explicit action, and your data is never sold or shared. Full details in our Privacy
Policy.
