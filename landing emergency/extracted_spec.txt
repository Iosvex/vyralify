1. Objective and strategy 
Business goal: maximum revenue from a freemium product. Signup is open to everyone at launch (no 
waitlist). This is the main Vyralify website, the page where people sign up and buy. 
The page has one job: get the visitor to click Start Free. Revenue comes from Free users who feel the 
product's value and upgrade to Pro or Elite. Two metrics: visitor to Free signup (this page), and Free to 
paid (the product, prepared by the pricing section). 
Two audiences, one page: beginners with no page who want to start one to earn, and existing creators 
who want to grow, automate and monetize faster. 
Positioning: one platform that takes a creator from zero to first sale. Brand promise: Everything You Need 
to Build, Grow & Monetize Your Instagram Business.

3. Global rules (apply to every section) 
From the uploaded specification (unchanged) 
•​
Theme: Dark hero + white body alternating pattern. Dark sections: bg #0A0A0A. White sections: 
bg #FFFFFF or #F9F9F9. 
•​
Green accent: #23E598 (primary CTA, highlights, active states) 
•​
Typography: Inter throughout. Headlines bold (700), body regular (400). 
•​
Max content width: 1200px, centered, with 80px side padding on desktop, 40px tablet, 20px 
mobile. 
•​
Primary CTA everywhere: “Start Free →” — green pill button, black text, no exceptions. 
•​
Secondary CTA: “See How It Works” or “Watch Demo” — ghost/outline style. 
•​
No credit card required microcopy always appears below primary CTA. 
•​
Navbar stays sticky — always visible on scroll. 
•​
Smooth scroll between sections. 
•​
All animations: subtle, purposeful, max 300ms. No bounce. No excessive motion. 
Added by the conversion rules 
•​
[+ Rule 1] The trust text under primary buttons in the hero, pricing and final CTA starts with the 
exact words Free plan · No credit card required. 
•​
[+ Rule 2] Copy rule: lead with the result, then name the tool. Example: not “AI assistant” but 
“Your personal Instagram growth co-pilot that analyzes your own metrics”. 
•​
[+ Rule 3] Wherever a section speaks to one audience, it carries a small chip: For Everyone, For 
Creators, For Beginners or For Scaling Creators. 
•​
[+ Rule 4] Every screenshot, phone mockup and floating card comes from the real product UI. No 
stock images, no decorative icons standing in for the product. 
•​
[+ Motion] All animation respects the device's reduce-motion setting. 
•​
[+ Accessibility] Visible focus ring on every interactive element; menus, accordions, toggles and 
the demo work with a keyboard.

7. After launch: the free mini tool (Rule 7) 
Why: a free, no-signup tool earns organic search traffic, lets visitors taste the product and turns them 
into qualified leads. It is the first growth experiment after launch. 
Two candidates (both reuse features already in Page Builder, so the build is small) 
1.​ Free 60-Second Niche Quiz: 4 to 5 quick questions, result is a recommended niche from the 8 
launch categories with its opportunity indicator. 
2.​ Free Instagram Bio Generator: the visitor enters their niche and goal and receives bio options. 
Flow: the visitor uses the tool with no account. To see the result they enter an email or continue with 
Google. They are told the result will also be saved to a free Vyralify account. Their answers carry into 
onboarding (niche pre-selected, bio saved), so signup continues what they started. 
Gate placement (to test): version A asks for email before showing the result (the rule). Version B shows 
the top result and asks for email to see the rest and save it. Measure both and keep the one with better 
signup and onboarding completion. 
Rules 
•​
A clear, unticked consent checkbox for marketing email, a link to the Privacy Policy and an easy 
unsubscribe. Collect only what is needed. 
•​
Protect cost and abuse: rate limits, bot protection and cached results, because each use may call 
AI. 
•​
Opportunity scores must be explainable; no promise that a niche will be profitable. 
•​
Dedicated pages (/tools/niche-finder, /tools/instagram-bio-generator) with one H1, a 
short FAQ and example outputs; each ends with Start Free. 
•​
Once live, add 'Free Tools' to the navbar and footer. 
Measure: tool start rate, completion rate, email capture rate, signup to onboarding completion, and Free 
to paid by source.
