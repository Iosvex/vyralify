Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026. Effective for all Vyralify members and visitors.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when registering for a membership, such as your full name, email address, country, and billing preference. When using our AI Creator tools or interacting in the community, we process tool prompts and message content securely to provide services.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized to authenticate your membership, grant access to curriculum assets, execute Groq AI completions, compute affiliate earnings, process recurring payments via Cashfree/Stripe, and maintain security across the platform.

3. Data Protection & Security

We enforce HTTPS encryption across all routes and utilize Google Cloud Firebase authentication and Firestore rules. Payment details (credit card numbers, UPI tokens, bank credentials) are handled exclusively by verified PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Cashfree & Stripe); Vyralify never stores raw card data.

4. Cookies & Tracking Tokens

We use essential browser storage (localStorage and session cookies) to persist authentication state, theme preferences, and cookie consent choices. You may decline non-essential cookies via our consent banner at any time.

5. Third-Party Integrations

We integrate with Groq Cloud (for AI completions), Cashfree Payments & Stripe (for billing), and Firebase Admin SDK. These providers operate under standard non-disclosure data processing agreements.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to or permanent deletion of your account data. To initiate a data erasure request, please email support@vyralify.in.
