User Data Deletion Instructions

Vyralify respects your privacy and gives you full control over your Instagram data. According to Meta Platform Rules, you may request the deletion of your user data associated with Vyralify at any time.

How to Request Immediate Data Deletion

  1. Go to your Facebook Account's Settings & Privacy ➔ Settings.
  2. Look for Apps and Websites to see all services connected to your Facebook/Instagram.
  3. Search for Vyralify in the list.
  4. Click Remove. This immediately revokes all Meta Graph API tokens and access.
  5. To completely purge all historical analytics records from our cloud database, send an email to vyralify.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Data" and your Instagram handle. Your records will be permanently erased within 24 hours.
