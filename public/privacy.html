Vyralify Privacy Standard

Privacy Policy for Vyralify

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

Vyralify ("we", "our", or "us") provides a creator operating system and Instagram growth analytics platform accessible at https://vyralify.in. We are dedicated to protecting your personal data and your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Instagram Creator or Business Account using Meta / Facebook OAuth, we receive authorized read-only permissions via Meta Graph API:

  • Basic Profile Information: Public Instagram username, handle, profile picture, and follower counts.
  • Media Metrics: Reach, impressions, and engagement rates for public posts and reels.
  • Account Email: Your registered email address used for workspace account security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly to:

  • Generate personalized AI bio optimization and niche positioning statements.
  • Render growth analytics and retention benchmark charts on your creator dashboard.
  • Provide automated keyword responses configured by you for direct messages and comments.

3. Data Protection & Storage

All API tokens and credentials exchanged through Meta Graph API are encrypted using standard security protocols. We never sell, rent, or lease your account information to third parties.

4. User Data Deletion

You can revoke Vyralify's access to your Instagram account at any time via your Facebook Settings -> Business Integrations. You can also request complete deletion of your stored data by visiting our User Data Deletion Page or emailing vyralify.app@gmail.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
