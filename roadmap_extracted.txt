1. Executive Overview & Single-Sided Positioning
Vyralify is an AI-powered SaaS operating system for Instagram creators and page owners. It serves a single-sided audience
through two unified onboarding paths: (1) Existing Creators looking to automate engagement, scale content velocity, and boost
revenue; and (2) Complete Beginners looking to launch a theme page from scratch, select a high-opportunity niche, and monetize
quickly.
Core User Journey:
BUILD (Page Builder) → DISCOVER & CREATE → AUTOMATE (Instagram Automation) →
MONETIZE (Store & Commerce) → MEASURE (Home Dashboard) → OPTIMIZE (Vyralify AI Assistant)
The 6 Core Modules:
1. Home • Overview, Page Analytics, Sales Tracker, Revenue Tracker, Recommendations, Quick
Actions
2. Vyralify AI Assistant • Ask Vyralify Anything, Multimodal Media Critique, Context Memory
3. Page Builder • Niche Selector, Import & Audit, Objectives, Profile Setup, Multi-Page, Demographics
4. Discover & Create • Trending Feeds, Viral DB, Niche Benchmarks, Competitor Tracking, Scripts,
30-Day Planner
5. Instagram Automation • DM Keywords, Comment Replies, Story DMs, Lead Collector, AI Inbox
6. Store & Commerce • Link-in-Bio Storefront, Winning Products Library, Digital Goods, Orders CRM
Explicitly OUT OF SCOPE:
• Marketing/landing website (built separately post-product)
• Brand-side/campaign marketplace features
• Video clipping / creator payout infrastructure
• Multi-user / team accounts / collaborator roles in v1
2. Global Architecture & Core Data Stores
The application is engineered around a reactive, multi-page context model where all analytics, content, automations, and store
products are strictly scoped to the active selected Instagram profile.
• Multi-Page Context Switcher: Persistent top-nav dropdown scoping all 6 modules. Switching pages instantly recalculates metrics
without full reload.
• Auth & Session Management: Firebase Client SDK with Email/Password, Google OAuth, Apple OAuth, 2FA, session tracking, and
device management.
• Soft-Lock Plan Gating: Gated features remain visible with lock badges, blurred previews, and contextual upgrade modals (never
broken buttons).
• Credit Metering Safety: Non-disruptive warnings at 80% usage and soft upgrade prompt at 100% (never cuts off in-flight responses).

Phase 1 — Global Foundation, App Shell, Auth & Dual Onboarding
FOUNDATION • CORE PREREQUISITE • APP SHELL
Establishes the entire application layout, persistent multi-page routing context, authentication security, and the branching
onboarding wizard for both beginner and existing page owners.
• Global State Architecture: Implement AuthContext (user, tier, credit counters), PageContext (multi-page context switcher scoping all
modules), PlanGatingContext (soft-lock UI with upgrade modal), and NotificationContext.
• Premium SaaS App Layout: High-craft obsidian/emerald navigation shell with persistent Left Sidebar (6 core panels +
Account/Settings), Top Bar with Multi-Page Dropdown, Cmd+K Search trigger, AI Credit pill meter, and notification bell.
• Authentication & Security: Email/password signup, Google & Apple OAuth simulation, email verification, password reset,
rate-limiting on failed attempts, ToS/Privacy acceptance, and explicit AI data-processing consent checkbox.
• Branching Onboarding Wizard: Step 1: Account setup → Step 2: Core Branching Question ('Do you already have a page or starting
from scratch?') → Path A: Meta OAuth connect & audit; Path B: Niche Selector Quiz & Opportunity Score → Step 3: Objective setting
→ Step 4: Land on Home with tailored checklist.
Phase 2 — Home Dashboard & Cross-Module Intelligence
MEASURE • FIRST SCREEN AFTER LOGIN • CORE OVERVIEW
The single-glance central dashboard summarizing page growth, content performance, storefront revenue, and proactive AI
recommendations.
• Overview Dashboard: 4 top KPI cards (Followers, 7-day Views, 30-day Revenue, Engagement Rate) with 7d/30d percentage
trends.
• Page Analytics: Reach, impressions, engagement trend curves, top performing and worst performing post breakdowns.
• Sales & Revenue Trackers: Live stream of recent storefront transactions, sales curves, and revenue breakdown by source (digital
products, services, affiliates).
• AI Recommendations Widget: Top 1–3 data-driven suggestions with 1-click deep-linking into the AI Assistant with pre-loaded
context.
• Quick Actions Bar & Zero-States: Instant buttons ('Create Content', 'View Store', 'Check Automations', 'Ask AI'). Empty accounts
display helpful onboarding states, never blank or broken charts.

Phase 3 — Page Builder & Official Niche Taxonomy System
BUILD • TAXONOMY & IDENTITY • AUDIT ENGINE
Where a page identity gets defined, audited, and optimized. All downstream content tools and AI recommendations filter by this
structured niche taxonomy.
• Official 8 Niche Taxonomy: Business & Money, Self-Improvement, Health & Fitness, Fashion & Beauty, Cars & Automotive, Travel
& Lifestyle, Entertainment, Sports (extensible enum with defined sub-niches).
• Niche Selector & AI Quiz: Guided quiz for beginners calculating profitability and opportunity scores per niche.
• Import Page & Meta Connect: Instagram account OAuth integration pulling live bio, profile pic, follower metrics, and running an
automated Page Audit (Strengths & Gaps scorecard).
• Positioning & Profile Setup: AI Bio Generator, positioning statement builder, and Instagram Story Highlights architecture.
• Multi-Page Management: Connected accounts dashboard with 1-click switching, disconnection, and Free tier 1-page soft-lock.
• Audience Demographics: Visual breakdown of follower age brackets, gender distribution, top geographic locations, and active
hours heatmap.
Phase 4 — Vyralify AI Assistant (Domain-Grounded Co-Pilot)
OPTIMIZE • CROSS-CUTTING AI BRAIN • STRATEGY ENGINE
A conversational AI assistant built specifically for Instagram growth mechanics, retention benchmarks, and live page data
reasoning.
• 'Ask Vyralify Anything' Chat Interface: Multi-turn, context-aware chat thread trained on retention spikes, hook patterns, and niche
benchmarks.
• Grounded in Real User Data: Directly references the active page's real analytics, top posts, follower growth, and store products in
answers.
• Conversation History & Search: Persistent chat history drawer with full keyword search and session naming.
• Multimodal Media Upload: Upload screenshots (analytics charts, competitor posts) or video thumbnails for visual AI critique and
scoring.
• Action Buttons in Responses: Generates interactive action buttons ('Create Script in Create', 'View in Discover', 'Add DM Trigger').
• Credit Metering & Warning Banners: Real-time credit deduction, 80% usage warning banner, and 100% upgrade prompt without
disrupting generated answers.

MONETIZE • CREATOR REVENUE • BIO COMMERCE
A high-converting single-page link-in-bio storefront (vyralify.in/u/username) where creators list digital products, services, and

• Orders & Customer CRM: Transaction log, fulfillment tracking, refund management, and CSV export.
• White-Label AI Resale: Elite-tier feature allowing users to rebrand and resell Vyralify AI on their own storefront.
Phase 8 — Account Settings, Billing & QA Hardening

• Timezone & Currency Preferences: Dynamic switching between INR (I) and USD ($) across all pricing, analytics, and revenue
trackers.
• Billing & Subscription Portal: Plan upgrade/downgrade flows (Razorpay/Stripe simulation), invoice history, and cancellation
handling.

Mandatory QA Verification Checklist
 Zero-States Everywhere: Every widget, graph, and list handles new accounts with zero data cleanly, showing guidance rather than
empty/broken charts.
 Soft-Lock Plan Gating: Free-tier restricted features always show blurred preview + lock icon + contextual upgrade dialog (never
broken buttons).

mid-sentence).

Discover, Automation, and Store.
 Meta API Compliance & Rate Limits: Automation queue implements backoff delay to prevent Instagram messaging rate limits and
account suspension risks.
 Dual Onboarding Integrity: Beginner path (Niche quiz) and Existing creator path (OAuth import) route flawlessly without leaking or
assuming page presence.

inspected in the browser, and presented to the founder for formal review and sign-off before proceeding to the subsequent phase.
